SHIPPING

How gear gets to you.

US shipping is a flat $5.99 added at checkout.

Checkout collects your name, email, phone, and shipping address. Printify ships from that address after payment.

Typical fulfillment is 5–12 business days after Printify accepts the order.

Sizes: Apparel runs S–2XL. Hats are S/M and L/XL. If you are between sizes, size up.
