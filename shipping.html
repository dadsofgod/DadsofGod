SHIPPING

How gear gets to you.

Checkout collects your name, email, phone, and shipping address. That information is attached to the PayPal order so we can fulfill it.

Listed prices are for the product. If postage is more than we can absorb, we will email you before we ship. Typical US fulfillment is 5–12 business days after payment, depending on stock.

Sizes: Apparel runs S–2XL. Hats are S/M and L/XL. If you are between sizes, size up. No published size chart yet — email a measurement question to contact@dadsofgod.com.
