SHIPPING

How gear gets to you.

We ship to US addresses only (50 states and DC). Shipping is a flat $5.99 added at checkout.

Checkout collects your name, email, shipping address, and an optional phone number. After PayPal confirms payment, we send the order to our print partner, Printify, which prints and ships it to that address.

Typical fulfillment is 5–12 business days after Printify accepts the order.

Sizes: Apparel runs S–2XL. The embroidered ballcap is one size. All gear is black. If you are between sizes, size up.
