OPERATIONS

How the money moves.

  1. Customer pays on dadsofgod.com (product + $5.99 US shipping, or a donation).
  2. PayPal deposits into the DoG PayPal Business account.
  3. Printify prints and ships from the paid order.
  4. Profit = what PayPal received minus Printify costs minus PayPal fees. 25% of that profit plus 100% of donations sits in the rescue bucket.
  5. Transfer to a named partner and publish the receipt on Impact.
