How the money moves.

  1. Customer pays on dadsofgod.com through the live PayPal buttons (gear or donate).
  2. PayPal deposits into the Dads of God PayPal Business account tied to the site's live client ID. That is the only automatic hop right now.
  3. Fulfill gear from the PayPal order notes (size + shipping address).
  4. Hold the promised cut in a separate bucket — 25% of merch revenue and 100% of donations. Do not spend that bucket on inventory or ads.
  5. Transfer to a named partner once they are vetted and can receipt the gift. Publish the transfer on the Impact page.
