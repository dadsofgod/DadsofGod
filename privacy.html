What we collect.

We collect what you type: contact form fields, cart contents stored in your browser, and shipping details you enter at checkout. Payments are processed by PayPal. Your phone number is required at checkout and is used only for shipping and delivery (for example, if the carrier needs to reach you). We do not use it for marketing. We do not sell your information.

Your browser stores the cart and your last shipping details locally so they survive a refresh. Clear site data to wipe it.
