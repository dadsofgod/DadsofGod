OUR STORY

We are dads who refuse to look away.

Dads of God was born from a simple conviction: every child deserves to be protected, and every father has a God-given duty to stand in the gap.

Too many men feel powerless against child trafficking and exploitation. We built something tangible — gear that funds the fight, and a brotherhood that will not look away.

25% of profits after print, shipping, and payment fees is reserved for vetted organizations fighting child trafficking. 100% of donations, after PayPal's processing fee, will go to vetted nonprofit partners fighting child trafficking, named before we claim any impact. Dads of God is a for-profit company, not a registered charity, so donations are not tax-deductible. Donations are held in Dads of God's PayPal account, labeled separately from product orders. We'll name those partners here once they're confirmed, and we will publish transfer totals when they are real — not before.

We are not a big corporation. We are fathers, husbands, brothers, and sons.

"We Were Made To Protect."

— The Dads of God Mission
